# Seizing the Agentic AI Advantage

**Source:** McKinsey & Company (QuantumBlack)
**Published:** June 13, 2025
**Original URL:** https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/seizing-the-agentic-ai-advantage

---

## Overview

McKinsey's QuantumBlack division released this strategic report examining what they term "the generative AI paradox" — the significant gap between widespread AI adoption and measurable business impact across enterprises. The report argues that the era of AI experimentation is over and that organizations must pivot to agentic AI integrated into core business workflows to unlock sustainable value.

## The Generative AI Paradox

- **~78% of companies** have adopted generative AI technology
- Yet **over 80% of adopters** report no measurable benefits on earnings
- **Only 1%** consider their AI strategy mature with clear advantages
- **~90% of vertical use cases** remain stalled at pilot stages due to technical, organizational, and cultural obstacles
- **Fewer than 10%** of use cases deployed ever make it past the pilot stage

### The Core Problem

There is a critical mismatch in how organizations deploy AI. Most focus on **horizontal applications** (enterprise-wide copilots and chatbots), which have scaled quickly but deliver "diffuse and hard-to-measure improvements." Meanwhile, **vertical use cases** targeting essential business functions demonstrate far greater transformative potential but face significant implementation barriers.

## Proposed Solution: Agentic AI

The report recommends autonomous AI agents as the path forward. These systems feature:

- **Memory and reasoning capabilities** — agents can retain context and make complex decisions
- **Planning and execution functions** — agents can break down multi-step tasks and execute them
- **Proactive collaboration** — rather than reactive responses, agents anticipate needs
- **Complex workflow automation** — agents can handle multi-step workflows that are too complex or uneconomical for humans

### Key Data Point on Untapped Potential

- **40% of workflows** include tasks that are too complex or uneconomical for humans to perform, but that agents could handle at scale

### Performance Benefits

Reported outcomes from agentic AI deployments include:

- **Cost and time reductions reaching 50%**
- Enhanced customer service performance
- Improved data analysis capabilities
- A **retail bank example** showed a potential **20–60% boost in productivity**, including a **30% faster credit turnaround**

## Implementation Framework: The Agentic AI Mesh

McKinsey introduces a modular architecture — the **Agentic AI Mesh** — combining customized and commercial agents to ensure interoperability and security at scale. The mesh enables rapid development, deployment, and management of interconnected AI agents.

## Key Recommendations

### 1. Blend Custom and Off-the-Shelf Agents

Realize the full potential of agentic AI by developing **custom-built agents** for high-impact processes — such as end-to-end customer resolution, adaptive supply chain orchestration, or complex decision-making — that are deeply aligned with the company's logic, data flows, and value creation levers. Complement these with commercial agents for standard tasks.

### 2. Build Scalable Foundations

Invest in flexible, interconnected networks of AI agents that enable their rapid development, deployment, and management, including:

- Modular, cloud-based architectures that can handle increasing complexity and volume
- Lower-complexity agents that empower nontechnical users to create and customize agents

### 3. Redesign Processes from Scratch

Organizations must "redesign processes from scratch" rather than integrating agents into existing workflows. This approach fully leverages agents' parallel execution, real-time adaptability, and large-scale personalization strengths.

### 4. Organizational Transformation

Organizations will need to:

- **Upskill the workforce** for new roles alongside AI
- **Adapt the technology infrastructure** for agent-ready architectures
- **Accelerate data productization** to feed agents quality data
- **Deploy agent-specific governance mechanisms** for oversight and compliance

## Four Pillars for Success

1. **People** — New skills and organizational roles required to work alongside agents
2. **Governance** — Aligning agent autonomy with business objectives and compliance
3. **Technology** — Scalable infrastructure to support agent ecosystems
4. **Data** — Quality assurance, access, and productization of enterprise data

## CEO-Level Action Required

The report emphasizes that "the moment has come to bring the gen AI experimentation chapter to a close — a pivot only the CEO can make." Organizations that move quickly toward the new agentic paradigm stand to gain significant competitive advantage; those that delay risk being left behind.

## Conclusion

McKinsey concludes that "the era of experimentation is over." Firms must adopt agents as active collaborators and fundamentally redesign their operating models to unlock sustainable value from AI investments. Success requires treating agentic AI not as a technology deployment but as an organizational transformation.
