# Reimagining Compliance With Agentic AI

## Overview

Oliver Wyman's latest report examines how agentic AI is transforming compliance functions at financial institutions. Unlike earlier AI tools handling isolated tasks, agentic AI operates as a semi-autonomous orchestrator that plans, acts, and coordinates complex compliance workflows with human oversight.

## Key Findings

**Operational Impact:**
- Automating up to 70% of manual work can enhance risk detection accuracy by up to 400%
- Enables continuous, comprehensive testing and real-time regulatory monitoring
- Shifts compliance teams from reactive task-doers to strategic advisers

**Current Adoption:**
The report presents data showing firms at various experimentation stages with agentic AI, though specific percentages aren't detailed in the provided excerpt.

## Four Strategic Priorities

1. **Framing ambitions and priorities** — Establish measurable objectives and select high-impact processes
2. **Reimagining workflows and roles** — Redesign activities to leverage AI for routine tasks while elevating humans toward strategic work
3. **Mitigating risks with strong controls** — Deploy disciplined rollout strategies with quality gates and robust traceability
4. **Building future-ready teams** — Reskill professionals into AI operations managers focused on oversight and continuous improvement

## Workforce Transformation

The report emphasizes that "AI-enabled execution teams will oversee and configure AI agent playbooks," shifting human talent toward critical adjudications and exception management rather than manual casework.

**Authors:** David Choi, David Carretero, Allen Meyer, Daniel Tannebaum, Frederic Thomas-Dupuis, and Kim-Mai Le
