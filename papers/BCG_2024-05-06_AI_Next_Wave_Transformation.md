# AI and the Next Wave of Transformation: Global Asset Management 2024

**Published:** May 6, 2024
**Source:** Boston Consulting Group
**URL:** https://www.bcg.com/publications/2024/ai-next-wave-of-transformation

## Overview

BCG's 2024 Global Asset Management Report examines how artificial intelligence can address mounting pressures facing the asset management industry. The analysis synthesizes survey data from asset managers controlling over $15 trillion in assets.

## Key Industry Challenges

Global assets under management reached nearly $120 trillion in 2023, recovering from prior-year declines. However, the sector faces structural headwinds:

- **Margin Compression:** Investors increasingly favor passive funds with lower fees despite rising operational costs
- **Product Innovation Stagnation:** New product launches have underperformed as investors maintain loyalty to established offerings
- **Growth Constraints:** Historically, market appreciation drove revenue; forward projections indicate slower appreciation will pressure profitability

## Strategic Framework: The Three Ps

BCG recommends asset managers pursue three interconnected strategies:

1. **Productivity** – Leverage AI for improved decision-making and operational efficiencies
2. **Personalization** – Deploy AI to create customized portfolios at scale
3. **Private Markets** – Expand alternative asset offerings using AI-enhanced deal evaluation

## AI Adoption Insights

Survey findings reveal significant momentum:

- **Majority Expectation:** Respondents anticipate "significant or transformative changes in the short term"
- **GenAI Deployment:** Two-thirds either plan to launch or are actively scaling at least one generative AI use case

## Critical Message

"Waiting is not an option when it comes to investing in AI. The technology is rapidly developing, and asset managers that do not start their journey now risk being left behind."

The report emphasizes that AI represents a transformational capability rather than merely an incremental enhancement, positioning early adopters for competitive advantage in an increasingly constrained market environment.
