# Why Generative AI Is A Game Changer For Leisure Travel

## Overview

Oliver Wyman's latest research demonstrates rapid growth in generative AI adoption among North American leisure travelers, with significant implications for the travel industry.

## Key Findings

**Adoption Growth:** "41% of the nearly 2,100 consumers from the United States and Canada told our March 2024 survey that they had recently used generative AI tools for travel inspiration or itinerary planning. That is up from 34% of respondents in our August 2023 survey."

**Future Intent:** Approximately 58% of all respondents and 82% of recent generative AI users indicated they would likely or very likely use these tools for travel planning again.

## Five Main Takeaways

1. Leisure travelers increasingly rely on generative AI for trip inspiration and itinerary planning
2. Younger consumers, elite loyalty members, and cruise enthusiasts show highest adoption rates
3. These tools effectively address existing pain points in travel planning
4. Consumers trust generative AI tools from familiar brands or those offering loyalty programs
5. The technology encourages complete trip bookings, increasing average trip value through ancillary services

## Strategic Implications

Travel companies adopting generative AI early can strengthen brand loyalty and boost direct bookings. However, delayed adoption carries financial risks—analysis suggests over $2 billion in potential online travel agency commissions are at stake for companies that neglect this technology.

**Authors:** Scot Hornick, Julien Boulenger, Grant Alport, and Lawrence Burka
