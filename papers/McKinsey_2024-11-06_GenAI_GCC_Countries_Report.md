# The State of Gen AI in the Middle East's GCC Countries: A 2024 Report Card

**Source:** McKinsey & Company (QuantumBlack)
**Published:** November 6, 2024
**Original URL:** https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-gen-ai-in-the-middle-easts-gcc-countries-a-2024-report-card

## Overview

This McKinsey report assesses the state of generative AI adoption and value creation across the six Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries. Based on an online survey of **140 executives from all six GCC countries across eight sectors**, the report finds that GCC organizations are adopting GenAI at rates significantly above global averages — but a large gap remains between adoption and actual value realization. A "fundamental rewiring of the organisation" is necessary for scaling adoption, though "realising full value from gen AI investments won't be instantaneous."

## Economic Impact Projections

- **GenAI could generate $21 billion to $35 billion annually** in economic value across GCC states
- This represents **1.7% to 2.8% of annual non-oil GDP** across GCC economies
- For context, global GenAI potential is estimated at **$2.6 trillion to $4.4 trillion** in annual economic value from 63 GenAI use cases
- Additional value from other AI technologies (machine learning, analytics, deep learning): **$15 billion** globally

## GCC Adoption Rates vs. Global Benchmarks

| Metric | GCC | Global |
|--------|-----|--------|
| GenAI adoption in at least one business function | **~75%** | 65% |
| Allocating 5%+ of digital budgets to GenAI | **57%** | 33% |
| Established implementation roadmaps for priority use cases | **50%** | 26% |

The GCC significantly outpaces global averages on all three dimensions of adoption, investment allocation, and strategic planning for GenAI.

## The Value Realization Gap

Despite high adoption rates, there is a significant gap between adoption and actual value creation:

- **Only 10 of 140 respondents (~7%)** reported generating over 5% of company earnings from GenAI
- This indicates "a sizable majority with substantial opportunities for growth"
- While 74% of GCC organizations are using GenAI in at least one function, only a fraction are deriving meaningful financial returns

### Characteristics of "Value Realisers"

The small group of companies successfully extracting value from GenAI share common traits:

- **Use proprietary or highly customized models** rather than off-the-shelf solutions
- **Invest in dedicated GenAI leaders** with clear organizational authority
- **Implement agile operating models** that allow rapid iteration
- **Adapt AI workflows across multiple functions**, not just isolated use cases

## Sector-Specific Opportunities

| Sector | Potential Annual Value |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Energy** | **$5–$8 billion** (highest potential) |
| Capital projects & infrastructure | Second highest |

## Implementation Focus Areas

GCC companies are prioritizing GenAI deployment in functions showing early returns on investment:

- **Sales**
- **Marketing**
- **Software engineering**
- **IT operations**

## Barriers to Scaling

Key obstacles impeding broader adoption across the GCC:

1. **Limited access to talent** — shortage of skilled AI professionals in the region
2. **Decentralized data management** — fragmented data architectures hinder AI model training and deployment
3. **Risk management hurdles** — regulatory and compliance uncertainty

## Risk Priorities

- **66% of respondents** prioritize **cybersecurity** as their top concern in GenAI implementations
- **Compliance** is a close second concern
- **Model accuracy and reliability** round out the top three risk areas

## Five Transformation Pillars for Scaling GenAI

McKinsey identifies five requirements for successfully scaling GenAI at the organizational level:

1. **Technology Infrastructure** — Robust, scalable platforms to support AI workloads
2. **Data Management and Governance** — Centralized, high-quality data pipelines with clear governance
3. **Talent Acquisition and Development** — Building and attracting AI-skilled workforce
4. **Operating Model Restructuring** — Agile, cross-functional models that embed AI into workflows
5. **Risk Management Frameworks** — Comprehensive approaches to cybersecurity, compliance, and model governance

## Key Conclusions

- The GCC region is well-positioned to capture significant value from GenAI, with adoption rates exceeding global averages
- However, only a limited number of organizations have begun to scale GenAI implementation and extract value from their investments
- Success requires moving beyond pilot programs to organization-wide transformation
- Energy, capital projects, and financial services represent the highest-value opportunities
- "A fundamental rewiring of the organisation" is necessary to move from experimentation to value realization at scale
