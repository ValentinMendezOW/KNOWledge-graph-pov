# The Widening AI Value Gap

**Publication Date:** September 30, 2025
**Source:** Boston Consulting Group
**URL:** https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/are-you-generating-value-from-ai-the-widening-gap

## Key Findings

BCG's research identifies a stark divide in AI value generation across organizations:

- **5% of companies** ("future-built") are successfully generating substantial AI value
- **35% of companies** are scaling AI and beginning to capture benefits
- **60% of companies** are realizing minimal returns despite significant investments

### Performance Gap

Future-built companies achieve dramatically superior results:
- **Five times greater revenue increases** from AI initiatives
- **Three times larger cost reductions** compared to lagging firms
- These gains are sufficient to drive meaningful shareholder returns

## What Sets Future-Built Companies Apart

The report identifies several distinguishing characteristics:

**Investment Strategy:** "Future-built companies plan to spend 26% more on IT and allocate up to 64% more of their budgets to AI in 2025," expecting compounded returns by 2028.

**AI Agents Focus:** Advanced firms allocate 15% of AI budgets to agentic AI, with one-third already deploying agents. Only 12% of scaling companies and virtually none of lagging firms use this technology.

**Value Creation Cycle:** These organizations reinvest AI gains into enhanced capabilities, creating a self-reinforcing cycle of innovation and efficiency.

## The Widening Gap

The emergence of agentic AI is accelerating the competitive divide. These autonomous systems currently represent 17% of total AI value in 2025 and are projected to reach 29% by 2028.

## Roadmap for Value Creation

BCG outlines a clear playbook for companies at different maturity levels:

**For Laggards:** Begin with strong executive commitment, establish a clear vision, and translate business objectives into comprehensive AI strategy.

**For Scaling Companies:** Invest in forward-looking capabilities like agentic AI innovation rather than relying solely on efficiency gains.

## Critical Takeaway

"Time is short. Future-built companies are pulling away, widening the value gap. Others need to move now," emphasizing urgency in AI transformation efforts.
