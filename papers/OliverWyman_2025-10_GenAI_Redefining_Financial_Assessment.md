# This Is How Generative AI Is Redefining Financial Assessment

**Author:** Tiago Rodrigues de Freitas
**Organization:** Oliver Wyman

---

## Overview

Generative AI is transforming credit risk assessment in financial institutions by moving beyond traditional linear credit scoring models. The technology enables banks to analyze unstructured data and identify subtle patterns that enhance predictive accuracy while streamlining manual processes.

---

## Key Capabilities

**Enhanced Data Analysis**
Traditional credit models rely on linear relationships between variables. Generative AI incorporates unstructured data sources including payment histories, transaction records, and e-commerce behavior to capture signals that improve model predictiveness and client discrimination.

**Process Efficiency**
The technology accelerates labor-intensive tasks, particularly in corporate lending where extensive documentation review is required. This reduces human error and time spent on manual analysis.

---

## Major Implementation Challenges

Three significant barriers confront financial institutions adopting these solutions:

1. **Technological Pace** - "the speed of change...makes clients defensive and hesitant to fully embrace new technologies"

2. **Model Explainability** - Understanding AI outputs remains difficult, though recent advances have improved interpretability

3. **Workforce Adaptation** - Staff require reskilling, though adoption barriers are expected to diminish over two to three years

---

## Practical Application: Credit Risk Assistant

GFT and Oliver Wyman developed an AI-powered tool helping credit analysts process annual reports, technical statements, and transactional data across multiple languages (French, Arabic, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese) without language-related complications.

---

## Future Outlook

The experts express confidence that AI will evolve from a supporting tool within existing processes to becoming the central driver of credit process redesign—representing an "inevitable" transformation in financial services.
