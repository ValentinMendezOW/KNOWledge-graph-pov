# The Economic Potential of Generative AI: The Next Productivity Frontier

**Source:** McKinsey & Company — McKinsey Digital / McKinsey Global Institute
**Date:** June 14, 2023
**URL:** https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier

---

## Overview

This landmark McKinsey Global Institute report provides the most comprehensive assessment of generative AI's economic impact to date. The research concludes that generative AI could add **$2.6 trillion to $4.4 trillion annually** to the global economy — an amount comparable to the entire GDP of the United Kingdom. The report analyzes 63 use cases across 16 business functions, examining 850 occupations and 2,100 work activities across 47 countries.

## Executive Summary

Generative AI has the potential to change the anatomy of work, augmenting the capabilities of individual workers by automating some of their activities. It could fundamentally alter knowledge work, which was previously considered relatively immune from automation. The technology's economic impact could roughly double when accounting for integration into broader software systems, reaching up to **$7.9 trillion annually**.

---

## Economic Impact

### Global Value Creation

- Generative AI could add **$2.6 trillion to $4.4 trillion annually** across the 63 use cases analyzed
- This would increase the total impact of all artificial intelligence by **15 to 40 percent**
- For comparison, the United Kingdom's 2021 GDP was **$3.1 trillion**
- Including the impact of embedding generative AI into software beyond the specific use cases, the total economic impact could approximately **double**, reaching **$7.9 trillion annually**
- AI software and services overall could generate between **$15.5 trillion to $22.9 trillion** in annual economic value by 2040

### Productivity Growth

- Automation enabled by generative AI could provide annual productivity growth of **0.5 to 3.4 percent** from 2023 to 2040
- Generative AI specifically contributes **0.1 to 0.6 percentage points** of that growth
- Combined with all automation technologies, productivity growth could reach **0.2 to 3.3 percentage points** annually
- The range depends on the rate of automation adoption and successful worker redeployment

### Investment Trends

- Equity investments in generative AI surged from **$5 billion (2022)** to **$36 billion (2023)**
- Revenue projections range from **$1.5 trillion** (conservative) to **$4.6 trillion** (high-end) by 2040

## Value Distribution by Business Function

Approximately **75% of the value** from generative AI use cases concentrates in four critical areas:

### 1. Customer Operations
- Could reduce human-serviced contacts by **up to 50%** in banking, telecommunications, and utilities
- One case study: A company with 5,000 customer service agents achieved a **14% increase in issue resolution per hour** and a **9% reduction in handling time**
- Productivity gains valued at **30-45% of current function costs**
- Technology particularly benefits less-experienced agents

### 2. Marketing and Sales
- Marketing productivity increases valued at **5-15% of global marketing spending**
- Sales productivity gains estimated at **3-5% of global sales expenditures**
- **Michaels Stores** increased email personalization from 20% to 95%, resulting in a **41% lift in SMS click-through rates** and **25% improvement in email campaigns**
- **Mattel** generated **four times more** product concept images using AI
- **Kellogg's** uses AI to identify trending recipes for social campaigns

### 3. Software Engineering
- **GitHub Copilot** users completed coding tasks **56% faster** than non-users
- McKinsey's developer study showed coding tasks completed **up to twice as fast**
- Code documentation: **45-50% time reduction**
- Code generation: **35-45% time reduction**
- Code refactoring: **20-30% time reduction**
- Teams experience improved job satisfaction alongside speed gains

### 4. Research and Development
- Foundation models complete lead identification in **weeks rather than months**
- Pharmaceutical companies report high success rates in Phase 3 clinical trials
- AI accelerates drug development timelines significantly
- Particularly valuable given that pharmaceuticals spend approximately **20% of revenues on R&D**

## Industry-Specific Impact

### Banking
- **$200 billion to $340 billion** annually in potential value
- **2.8-4.7% of annual revenues** in productivity gains
- Key applications: virtual expertise, code acceleration, regulatory compliance automation

### Retail and Consumer Packaged Goods
- **$400 billion to $660 billion** annually in potential value
- **1.2-2.0% of annual revenues** in productivity gains
- Key application: hyperpersonalization
- One European telecom company achieved a **40% lift in response rates** and **25% reduction in deployment costs** by expanding from 4 customer segments to 150 personalized segments

### Life Sciences
- **2.6-4.5% of annual revenues** in potential impact
- Particularly significant given high R&D spending ratios (~20% of revenues)
- Key applications: drug discovery acceleration, clinical trial optimization

### High Tech
- Among the industries seeing the biggest percentage-of-revenue impact
- Substantial benefits from gen AI's software development acceleration capabilities

## Workforce Transformation

### Automation Potential
- Current generative AI and other technologies can automate work activities absorbing **60 to 70 percent of employees' time** today
- Previous estimates had suggested approximately **50%**
- Technology understands natural language for activities representing **25% of total work time**

### Revised Automation Timeline
- Half of today's work activities could be automated between **2030 and 2060**
- Midpoint estimate: **2045** — roughly **a decade earlier** than previous estimates
- Adoption accelerates faster in developed nations with higher wage structures

### Impact on Knowledge Workers (Reverse Skill Bias)
- Unlike previous technological disruptions, generative AI **disproportionately affects higher-educated knowledge workers**
- Workers in the **highest and second-highest income quintiles** face the greatest automation potential
- Professionals in education, law, technology, and the arts face near-term automation pressure
- This represents a fundamental shift from previous automation waves that primarily targeted physical work

### Employee vs. Leadership Perception Gap
- **94% of employees** report familiarity with generative AI tools
- C-suite leaders estimate only **4%** use AI for at least 30% of daily work
- Reality: **13% of employees** self-report this usage level — **three times higher** than leadership estimates
- **47% of employees** believe they will use AI for over 30% of tasks within one year, versus only **20% of leaders**

---

## Implementation Status and Challenges

### Maturity Gap
- Only **1% of business leaders** describe generative AI rollouts as "mature" — fully integrated and driving substantial business outcomes
- **92%** plan increased AI investments over three years

### Current ROI Reality
- Only **19% of executives** report revenue increases exceeding 5% from generative AI
- **36%** report no revenue change
- **23%** observe any favorable cost changes
- Yet **87%** expect revenue growth within three years, with **51%** anticipating increases over 5%

### Five Critical Implementation Barriers
1. **Leadership alignment** across business domains
2. **Cost uncertainty** for scaled implementations
3. **Workforce planning** complexity
4. **Supply chain dependencies** on specialized hardware and geopolitical considerations
5. **Explainability demands** for regulated environments

### Success Requirements
- Worker skill development investments
- Occupational transition support
- Risk management strategies
- Business process redesign for reskilling programs
- Human oversight of AI outputs

## Research Methodology

- **63 use cases** analyzed across **16 business functions**
- **850 occupations** examined
- **2,100 detailed work activities** assessed
- **47 countries** covered, representing more than **80% of the global workforce**
- Analysis builds on decades of McKinsey Global Institute research on automation and work

## Technological Context

- The pace of AI advancement exceeded previous projections
- McKinsey previously identified **2027** as the earliest year for median human performance in natural language understanding, but this milestone was reached **four years ahead of schedule** in 2023
- By mid-2024, Google's Gemini 1.5 was processing **two million tokens**
- OpenAI's o1 model demonstrated advanced reasoning capabilities
- Multimodal capabilities became standard across leading models
