# Reinventing Business with GenAI: Productivity Gains and Workforce Transformation

**Published:** February 20, 2025
**Source:** PwC Malta, based on PwC's 28th Annual Global CEO Survey
**Survey Scope:** 4,701 CEOs surveyed across 109 countries and territories (October 1 - November 8, 2024)

---

## Overview

This PwC publication examines how generative AI is driving early productivity gains and transforming the workforce, drawing on data from PwC's 28th Annual Global CEO Survey. The report highlights the need for leaders to prioritize upskilling and reskilling employees to capture the growth and value-creation potential that GenAI offers to businesses.

---

## GenAI Productivity Gains

### Realized Impact (Past 12 Months)
- **56%** of CEOs report that GenAI has resulted in efficiencies in how employees use their time
- **34%** saw profitability increases from GenAI investments
- **32%** experienced revenue increases
- In 2024, 46% of CEOs had predicted profitability improvements, but only 34% realized those gains — highlighting a gap between expectations and outcomes

### Expected Impact (Next 12 Months)
- **49%** of CEOs expect profitability improvements from GenAI in the coming year

### Trust Challenge
- Only **one-third** of CEOs have high confidence in embedding AI into key processes
- Trust remains a significant barrier to scaled adoption

---

## GenAI Integration Plans (Next Three Years)

- **47%** of CEOs plan to integrate AI into technology platforms
- **41%** intend to integrate AI into core business processes
- **30%** have plans for new products and service development using AI

---

## Workforce Transformation

### Headcount Impact
- **42%** of CEOs expect to increase headcount by 5%+ in the next 12 months
- **17%** expect decreases
- More CEOs say GenAI has **increased** headcount (17%) than **decreased** it (13%)
- **48%** of smaller companies (<$100M revenue) plan headcount growth

### Highest Growth Sectors
- Technology: **61%** planning headcount increases
- Real Estate: **61%**
- Private Equity: **52%**
- Pharma/Life Sciences: **51%**

### Skills Development Imperative
- **69%** of CEOs believe GenAI will require most of their workforce to develop new skills in the next three years
- **76%** of workers expect GenAI to provide opportunities to learn new skills

---

## Worker Perspective: GenAI Impact by Usage Frequency

Data from PwC's 2025 Global Workforce Hopes & Fears Survey (49,843 workers across 48 countries) reveals stark differences based on AI usage:

### Productivity
- **92%** of daily GenAI users report productivity gains vs. **58%** of infrequent users

### Job Security
- **58%** of daily users see improved job security vs. **36%** of infrequent users

### Salary Impact
- **52%** of daily users experience salary increases vs. **32%** of infrequent users

### Optimism About Future Roles
- **69%** of daily AI users feel optimistic about their roles over the next 12 months
- **51%** of infrequent users share this optimism
- **44%** of non-users express similar confidence

### Adoption Rates
- Only **14%** use GenAI daily (slight increase from 12% in 2024)
- **54%** have used AI for their role in the past 12 months
- Merely **6%** employ agentic AI daily

---

## Access to Learning and Upskilling

- **72%** of senior executives feel equipped for learning and development
- **66%** of managers report adequate resources
- Only **51%** of non-managers feel they have necessary learning support
- **75%** of daily AI users feel resourced for professional growth vs. **59%** of infrequent users

---

## Business Reinvention Imperative

### Viability Concerns
- **42%** of CEOs believe companies won't remain viable beyond 10 years without reinvention
- **63%** have taken significant reinvention actions in the last five years
- **38%** have begun competing in new sectors within five years
- **34%** report new sector competition represents over 20% of company revenue

### Resource Reallocation Challenges
- Approximately **50%** of companies reallocate 10% or less of financial and human resources annually
- Two-thirds reallocate less than 20%
- Only **7%** of revenue over five years comes from distinct new businesses

---

## CEO Economic Outlook

- **59%** expect global economic growth to increase over next 12 months (up from 38% last year and 18% two years ago)

### Top Risks Cited by CEOs
- Macroeconomic volatility: **29%**
- Inflation: **27%**
- Geopolitical conflict: **41%** (Middle East), **34%** (Central/Eastern Europe)
- Cyber risk: **27%** (Western Europe)

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **GenAI is delivering real but uneven productivity gains** — over half of CEOs see efficiency improvements, though profitability gains lag expectations
2. **GenAI is creating more jobs than it eliminates** — more CEOs report headcount increases than decreases due to AI
3. **The skills gap is urgent** — 69% of CEOs see workforce reskilling as a near-term necessity, yet access to learning resources is unequal
4. **Daily AI users reap disproportionate benefits** — in productivity, pay, job security, and career optimism
5. **Business reinvention is existential** — 42% of CEOs believe their companies must fundamentally transform within 10 years to survive

*Sources: PwC 28th Annual Global CEO Survey (4,701 CEOs, 109 countries, October-November 2024); PwC 2025 Global Workforce Hopes & Fears Survey (49,843 workers, 48 countries). Published by PwC Malta, February 20, 2025.*
