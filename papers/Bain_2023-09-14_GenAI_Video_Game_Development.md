# Generative AI Will Contribute to More Than Half of Video Game Development Within Next 5 to 10 Years, Finds Bain & Company

**Date:** September 14, 2023
**Source:** Bain & Company Press Release

---

## Overview

Bain & Company released research indicating that generative AI will support more than half of video game development processes over the next five to ten years. The study surveyed 25 gaming executives globally to understand expectations and barriers surrounding AI adoption in the industry.

---

## Key Findings

**Executive Expectations:**
- Most respondents believe generative AI will enhance game quality and accelerate development timelines
- Only **20%** expect cost reductions from AI implementation
- **60%** of executives do not anticipate significant talent impact and doubt AI will ease the industry's talent shortage

**Scope of Impact:**
- Generative AI is expected to contribute to **more than half** of the video game development process within the next 5 to 10 years
- The technology's potential impact on gaming significantly exceeds that of previous technologies like virtual reality and cloud gaming, positioning it as transformative for the sector

---

## Use Case Evolution

The research indicates a shift in AI application focus. Currently, generative AI is primarily used during preproduction, but over the coming decade, executives expect broader adoption during production stages, including:

- **Story generation and NPC development:** Creating narratives and developing nonplayable character behaviors and dialogue
- **Game asset creation:** Generating visual assets, environments, textures, and 3D models
- **Live game operations:** Managing and updating live service games with AI-driven content and optimization
- **User-generated content systems:** Enabling players to create content using AI-powered tools

---

## Implementation Barriers

Executives identified several obstacles to AI adoption:

1. **System integration challenges** (primary concern)
2. **Data training requirements**
3. **Insufficient technical capabilities**
4. **Regulatory and legal uncertainty**
5. **Implementation costs**
6. **Lack of comprehensive AI strategies**
7. **Talent retention issues** in an evolving landscape

---

## Strategic Recommendations

Bain outlined four key approaches for gaming companies:

### 1. Disciplined Implementation
Establish clear ambitions, governance frameworks, and guardrails to optimize ROI and mitigate risks. Companies should set measurable targets and build accountability into their AI programs.

### 2. Player-Centric Focus
Prioritize user experience over content volume. The research cautions that more content doesn't necessarily improve gameplay. Companies should use AI to enhance the quality and depth of player interactions rather than simply increasing the quantity of content.

### 3. Strategic Partnerships
Carefully evaluate building capabilities in-house versus partnering with established or specialized ecosystem players. The rapidly evolving AI landscape makes it impractical for most gaming companies to develop all capabilities internally.

### 4. Organizational Transformation
Recognize that AI adoption extends beyond technology. It requires process redesign, new workflows, and enterprise architecture planning. Companies must invest in change management and workforce development alongside technology deployment.

---

## Industry Context

The gaming industry faces unique opportunities and challenges with generative AI:

- **Development timelines** for major titles have grown significantly, making AI-driven acceleration particularly valuable
- **Rising development costs** create pressure to find efficiency gains, though only a minority of executives see AI primarily as a cost reduction tool
- **Creative potential** of AI tools could fundamentally alter the design process, enabling more experimentation and iteration
- **Competitive dynamics** will shift as AI-savvy studios gain advantages in development speed and content quality
