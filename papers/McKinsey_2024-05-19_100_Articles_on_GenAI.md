## Overview

McKinsey has published more than 100 articles on generative AI to help organizations turn the promise of gen AI into sustainable business value. This curated collection serves as a comprehensive guide for leaders — from initial curiosity to full-scale, results-driven adoption.

The collection includes insights on:
- Scaling AI inside large organizations
- Applying AI for social good
- Addressing risks and ethical considerations
- Industry-specific perspectives (energy and materials, real estate, life sciences, high tech, banking)
- How CIOs can get from pilot to scale
- The tech's potential impact on productivity

The collection features work by **Alex Singla** and **Alexander Sukharevsky**, global co-leaders of **QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey**.

## Headline Economic Finding

Generative AI could add the equivalent of **$2.6 trillion to $4.4 trillion** in value to the global economy annually. This would increase the total impact of all AI by **15 to 40 percent** beyond the $11 trillion to $17.7 trillion that non-generative AI and analytics could already unlock.

## Key Articles in the Collection

### Foundational Research

#### 1. The Economic Potential of Generative AI: The Next Productivity Frontier (June 2023)
The flagship research report underpinning the collection. McKinsey examined **63 use cases** across **16 business functions** where generative AI can address specific business challenges. The analysis covered more than **850 occupations** and **2,100 detailed work activities** across multiple countries.

**Key findings:**
- About **75% of the value** that generative AI use cases could deliver falls across four areas:
  1. **Customer operations** — automating interactions, improving resolution
  2. **Marketing and sales** — personalized content, campaign optimization
  3. **Software engineering** — code generation, testing, documentation
  4. **R&D** — accelerating drug discovery, design iteration, research synthesis
- For retail alone, generative AI could contribute roughly **$310 billion** in additional value
- In high tech, the bulk of potential value comes from increased speed and efficiency of software development

#### 2. The State of AI in 2023: Generative AI's Breakout Year

## Recommendations for Organizations

Based on the collective insights from the 100 articles, McKinsey recommends:

1. **Start with value, not technology** — Identify high-value use cases aligned with business strategy before choosing tools
2. **Move beyond pilots** — Invest in the organizational capabilities (talent, data, architecture) needed for scale
3. **Govern proactively** — Establish responsible AI frameworks before problems emerge
4. **Upskill the workforce** — Prepare employees for augmented roles rather than treating AI as pure automation
5. **Engage leadership** — Make generative AI a board-level and C-suite priority
6. **Balance speed with safety** — Deploy quickly but with appropriate guardrails
7. **Think ecosystem** — Consider industry-specific applications and partnership opportunities
8. **Measure rigorously** — Track ROI and impact metrics to justify continued investment
