# Meet LenAI — A New Generative AI Tool

## Article Overview

This article features a conversation between Paul Beswick (Chief Information Officer, Marsh McLennan) and Vivek Sen (Head of Oliver Wyman Digital in the Americas) discussing LenAI, an internal generative AI platform developed by Marsh McLennan.

---

## Key Content

### About LenAI

LenAI represents Marsh McLennan's proprietary generative AI tool designed to provide employees with a secure, low-cost environment for experimentation and problem-solving. The initial version was deployed in a single day, though this milestone built upon years of foundational infrastructure work.

### Core Capabilities

The platform offers several primary functions:
- Document upload, summarization, and Q&A capabilities
- Meeting summarization
- Data extraction from documents
- Draft generation for emails and presentations
- Internet search integration to minimize hallucination issues

### Development Approach

Rather than restricting access, Marsh McLennan implemented a phased rollout strategy:

1. **Initial phase**: Public service experimentation with strict guardrails using non-sensitive data
2. **API-based access**: Technical teams explored specific use cases through Microsoft's private OpenAI model deployment
3. **Broad rollout**: Deployment across the entire organization (targeting 90,000 employees)

### Current Adoption

Approximately 12,000 employees were actively using the platform at the time of this discussion, with expansion to the full 90,000-person workforce planned.

### Business Applications

According to Sen: "We're using it in our private capital teams to industrialize...digital intelligence work for private equity firms."

### Security & Risk Management

The successful rapid deployment relied on pre-existing security infrastructure and compliance standards already embedded in Marsh McLennan's technology platform. Beswick emphasized that "we'd already built a lot of the enabling components...in a way that is secure."

### Lessons Learned

Key takeaways from the implementation include:

- Building flexible, future-proof architecture allowing backend model updates
- Establishing rapid deployment capabilities (applications can launch in approximately 25 minutes)
- Cultivating organizational risk management culture
- Enabling broad experimentation across hundreds of concurrent projects

### Industry Context

Beswick noted that many larger organizations remain challenged by security and governance considerations when implementing similar technologies, though adoption is expected to accelerate.
