# How Generative AI Will Supercharge Productivity

**Published:** August 22, 2023
**Authors:** Roy Singh and Dunigan O'Keeffe (Bain & Company)
**Type:** Snap Chart
**Source:** Bain & Company

---

## Overview

Generative AI has reached an inflection point, offering tangible benefits across industries and business functions. This Bain & Company snap chart analysis examines how generative AI can enhance worker productivity, finding that the impact varies significantly depending on sector and role. Early adopters are already capturing measurable value, while many organizations face integration challenges.

---

## Core Finding

Generative AI holds opportunities to transform worker productivity, but to different degrees across functions and industries. Bain estimates that approximately **20% of all work tasks** could be completed faster and at equal quality with assistance from generative AI.

---

## Productivity Gains by Function

### Software Development
- Developer organizations are saving **15% to 40%** on code generation and documentation
- **30% to 50%+** time savings on refactoring, select testing, and debugging use cases
- Coders using AI assistance are up to **40% more productive**
- Organizations achieving comprehensive transformation across the software lifecycle see productivity gains of **25-30%**, far above the 10% from basic code assistants alone
- 57% of software engineers anticipate **20% or better** productivity gains from generative AI over the next two years

### Marketing
- Marketing use cases can deliver productivity gains of **30% to 40%**
- Time required to create marketing content has decreased by up to **50%** in some organizations

### Customer Service
- AI integration reduces customer support response times by approximately **one-third**
- AI-powered self-help tools have reduced customer contacts by **20%** in implementations such as TurboTax product support
- Automated message response systems (e.g., UPS's MeRA) reduce time spent on email handling by **50%**

### Overall Business Impact
- Leaders using AI were improving EBITDA by **10% to 25%**
- Companies like Intuit reported a **15% average productivity gain** from generative AI trials, with certain tasks reporting even higher gains

---

## Key Themes

### 1. Variability Across Functions and Industries
Not all roles or sectors benefit equally. Functions involving high volumes of text generation, code writing, data analysis, and customer interaction see the largest gains. Industries with significant knowledge work components benefit disproportionately.

### 2. Early Adoption Advantage
Organizations that began integrating generative AI early are already capturing measurable value. The gap between early adopters and laggards is widening as early movers refine their approaches and scale successful pilots.

### 3. Integration Challenges
Despite significant potential, many companies struggle with practical implementation of AI capabilities. Barriers include:
- Data quality and accessibility
- Workforce readiness and training
- Process redesign requirements
- Governance and compliance considerations

### 4. Beyond Simple Automation
The largest productivity gains come not from automating individual tasks but from comprehensively rethinking workflows and processes. Companies that redesign end-to-end processes around AI capabilities see substantially higher returns than those deploying AI as a point solution.

---

## Implications

The research underscores that generative AI is not a uniform productivity multiplier. Organizations must carefully assess which functions and workflows stand to benefit most, prioritize those areas for investment, and build the organizational capabilities needed to scale successful implementations across the enterprise.
