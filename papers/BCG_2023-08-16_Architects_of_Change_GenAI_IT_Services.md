# Architects of Change: Generative AI's Role in Revolutionizing IT Services

**Publication Date:** August 16, 2023
**Source:** Boston Consulting Group
**Authors:** Rajiv Gupta, Sumit Sarawgi, Vikash Jain, and Sambhav Jain

---

## Overview

This BCG report examines how generative AI is transforming the IT services and Business Process Management (BPM) sectors. The analysis addresses competing narratives about the technology's impact, from claims of widespread job displacement to the emergence of entirely new professional categories.

## Key Focus Areas

The report explores several critical dimensions:

**Industry Disruption and Transformation**
The publication investigates how generative AI may fundamentally reshape the industry landscape, challenging conventional business models and operational approaches.

**Employment Impact**
The analysis acknowledges various perspectives on workforce implications, acknowledging both concerns about role elimination and opportunities for new positions.

**Impact on IT Services Organizations**
The report identifies how generative AI affects:
- Core business processes and workflows
- Expansion of traditional service offerings
- Development of novel service capabilities
- Evolution of commercial and pricing structures

**Strategic Imperatives**
The authors outline critical priorities for IT and BPM services firms to successfully navigate and leverage generative AI advancements.
